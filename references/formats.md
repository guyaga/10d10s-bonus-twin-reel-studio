# Reel formats that worked

Each one was built and published with this pipeline. Use them as the starting shape, not as a template to fill in.

## The list: "N signs of X" (60–100 s)

- **Hook (0–8 s):** name the type of person or thing, say the viewer has met it, and promise a way to spot it. A big number sits behind the twin.
- **One beat per sign:** the red counter block flips to the number with a short label, the sign in one line, then the real problem or the "why" in one or two plain sentences, with one strong visual (a sticker from the hand, a crossed-out chip, a stamp).
- **The turn:** "so who can you trust / what do you do instead", with the positive version.
- **End:** a punchline, or the CTA.
- **Watch for:** each sign has to be clear on its own. A label without the "why" has no punch.

## The news explainer (50–70 s)

- **Hook:** the headline as a scare or a surprise, with a LIVE ticker along the bottom.
- **What happened:** in plain words, with headline cards (source, date) and one cutaway plate.
- **Why it matters to you:** who is affected, and what it costs them.
- **What to do today:** 1–3 concrete steps (a typed chat bubble or a checklist).
- **Watch for:** fact-check every number against the original source before writing the script, and say when it happened.

## The personal story / "AI did this for me" (45–60 s)

- **Hook:** the result first, as a thumbnail-worthy frame (a phone lock screen with a notification, a calendar, a receipt).
- **The problem:** what kept going wrong before (a countdown, a sold-out counter).
- **What I built:** one chat, no code. Show the request in a chat bubble.
- **How it works:** a simple flow, 3–4 steps, with a counter for the time or money it saves.
- **The proof:** real data (anonymised), shown as tiles or a chart.
- **The insight + a question to the viewer:** "what small thing do you put off every week?"
- **Loop:** end on the same frame the reel opened with.

## The testimonial (30–45 s)

- **Hook:** the strongest line of the message, big.
- **The message:** a screenshot recreated as a chat card, highlighted line by line, held long enough to read. Hide the sender's name, number and photo.
- **Context:** who it's from (generic: "a member", "a client"), and what they did.
- **End:** the CTA.

## The tip (30–50 s)

- **Hook:** the mistake most people make.
- **The tip:** one change, shown as a before/after (two chat bubbles or two results).
- **Why it works:** one sentence.
- **Try it:** the exact words to copy, on screen long enough to screenshot.
